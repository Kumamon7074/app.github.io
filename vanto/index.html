A few things to know

Does Vanto include movies or TV shows?

Vanto does not provide, host, or sell media content. Connect and play only content you are authorized to access.

Which media sources can I connect?

Connect Emby, Jellyfin, and Plex media servers that you manage or are authorized to access, plus SMB and WebDAV file sources. On supported platforms, you can also import videos from your device. Browse posters, seasons and episodes, cast, media details, and NFO metadata in one clear interface.

Is NFO editing free?

Edit NFO for free on iPhone, iPad and Mac with a writable SMB or WebDAV source. Apple TV displays NFO but does not edit it.

Where does my data go?

Media stays with your sources; NFO is saved to your NAS. Supported settings and user state can sync through iCloud. See the privacy policy for diagnostics and purchases.
